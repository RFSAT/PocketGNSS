# Google Play listing and declarations

## Store listing

**App name:** PocketGNSS

**Short description (80):** Manage RTK GNSS receivers: centimetre position, NTRIP, survey and stake-out.

**Full description:**

PocketGNSS connects your phone to an RTK GNSS receiver, such as the receiver RFSAT Limited developed for precision
agriculture in the EU AgriBIT project (u-blox ZED-F9P / ZED-F9R), over USB-OTG, Bluetooth or the network, and shows
its position to the centimetre.

• Live position, fix type (autonomous, SBAS, RTK float, RTK fixed), accuracy, satellites in view and used, DOP,
  signal levels per constellation (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou) and a sky plot
• NTRIP client: RTK corrections from your provider through the phone's data connection, with mountpoint list,
  RTCM message monitor and base-station distance
• Map with satellite, street and terrain layers; track coloured by fix type; saved points
• Survey points by averaging, with standard deviations, CEP and 2DRMS, and comparison with a known coordinate
• Stake out saved points to the centimetre
• Record sessions and export NMEA, CSV, GPX, KML and GeoJSON
• Configure the u-blox NMEA messages, ports and rate (1-20 Hz) without a PC
• Open the receiver's own web settings over its Wi-Fi hotspot
• Time-to-fix and data-rate tests
• Provide the RTK position to other apps (mock location) or over a local NMEA TCP server
• Phone RTK: use the phone's own GNSS chip with your NTRIP corrections (accuracy depends on the phone; best with
  dual-frequency phones)
• Phone raw GNSS test: does this phone's GNSS chip deliver carrier phase for RTK? Record RINEX and the caster's
  RTCM stream for post-processing (RTKLIB, MATLAB)
• Built-in simulator: try every screen and tool without a receiver

Made by RFSAT Limited, a non-profit R&D company in Dublin and Athens. Developed with support from Claude AI (Anthropic).

## App content declarations

**Foreground service types** (Play Console > App content > Foreground services):
* `connectedDevice`: keeps the USB / Bluetooth link to the user's GNSS receiver open while recording, forwarding
  NTRIP corrections or serving NMEA with the screen off. User-initiated; ongoing notification with Stop.
* `location`: keeps providing the receiver's position as mock location to other apps (e.g. agricultural guidance
  and field mapping) with the screen off, and keeps a user-started phone raw GNSS recording (20–30 min static
  session) running with the screen off. User-initiated; ongoing notification with Stop.
  Video of the feature may be requested: Setup > Outputs > Publish as phone location, then lock the screen.

**Permissions:** `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` (the location foreground service type, and the phone's own GNSS when the
user chooses it as a source or runs the phone raw GNSS test / recording; positions and measurements stay on the
phone unless the user shares a file), `ACCESS_MOCK_LOCATION` (provider for the mock location feature, active only when the user
selects the app in Developer options), `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT` (paired receiver), `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`.
No background location access is requested.

**Data safety:**
* Location: shared with a third party only when the user enables NTRIP and the caster needs GGA (the user's own
  correction provider; required for the functionality). Not collected by the developer.
* Personal info, app activity, identifiers: none collected.
* Data is encrypted in transit when the user enables TLS for the caster; map tiles over HTTPS.
* Users can delete their data in the app (Log tab) or by uninstalling.

**Privacy policy URL:** publish `docs/privacy-policy.md` on rfsat.com and enter the address.

**Target audience:** 18+ (professional / agricultural tool). **Ads:** none.

**Reviewer access:** no account needed; choose Setup > Receiver connection > Simulator.
